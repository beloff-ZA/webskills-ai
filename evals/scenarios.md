# Cross-industry evaluation scenarios
Test this same skill, unchanged, against:
1. Irreverent embroidered office apparel with original slogans and a small catalogue.
2. Premium jewellery with detailed product materials and trust requirements.
3. Specialist running retail with intent-based filters and equipment specifications.
4. Industrial replacement parts store with critical SKU compatibility and B2B quoting.
5. Children's educational products store with approachable but accessible communication.
6. Existing conservation non-profit with established assets and donation flows.
7. A blank concept: only 'I want an online shop for locally made objects.'
8. A specific pre-existing website needing a minor repair, where a redesign would violate scope.

## Evaluation
For each scenario collect: project facts/unknowns, rationale, distinct creative direction, hierarchy, functional task success, responsiveness/technical evidence, and anti-AI audit. Compare outputs: if all share the same hero/card/nav rhythm, the skill fails its diversity objective. Do not promote project-specific choices into universal rules.
