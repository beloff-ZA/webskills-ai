# External inspiration library
These URLs are optional browsing starting points, not required dependencies, approved assets, suggested themes, or licences. Their content may change; verify current pages when using them.

## Graphic and art direction
- https://dribbble.com/tags/ui-ux-website-design — concepts and individual interface explorations; often not functional.
- https://www.behance.net/ — extended identity and editorial projects; distinguish presentation mockups from live implementations.
- https://www.siteinspire.com/ — curated visual approaches and layouts.

## Experimental digital craft
- https://www.awwwards.com/ — creative visual and interaction work; examine usability/performance trade-offs.
- https://www.cssdesignawards.com/ — visually ambitious examples; judge on more than awards.

## Interface patterns and shopping journeys
- https://mobbin.com/ — interface pattern and flow references; access may be restricted.
- https://www.karmaclothing.co.za/ — corporate clothing identity and product/service communication.
- https://barron.com/ — broad catalogue and customisation retail needs.
- https://productionportugal.com/ — editorial and immersive production storytelling; not an e-commerce standard.
- https://palaceskateboards.com/ — brand-led campaign expression; do not imitate signature identity.
- https://www.achillesheel.co.uk/ — specialist commerce taxonomy and editorial merchandising.

## Usage
Select sources based on the current design question. Seek contrasts across industries, composition types, cultures, visual eras and functional needs. Examine source intent and visual technique, not exact pixels. Prefer live websites for behavioural claims; visuals alone cannot verify code quality, mobile behaviour, performance or accessibility. Record source URLs separately when research informed a design. Never download and reuse copyrighted media or copy CSS/code without permission.
