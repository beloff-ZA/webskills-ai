# Product Websites & Beverage Inspiration — research notes (11 Nov 2026)
Sources are research references, not licenses, templates, endorsement or a list to clone. Re-check URLs and current appearance at use time. Separate LIVE sites from CONCEPT projects. Behaviour, accessibility and performance of sites were not audited: deductions below are drawn from publicly accessible descriptions and pages.

## Live premium cider and sparkling beverage
1. Maison Sassy — https://maison-sassy.co.uk/ — LIVE. Normandy cider framed as modern aperitif; credible range/heritage/occasion storytelling. Useful: bridge approachable and upscale. Avoid copying château aesthetics or product-story wording.
2. Aspall Cyder — https://www.aspall.co.uk/ — LIVE. Premium heritage, differentiated range and craftsmanship. Useful: hierarchy of product types and verifiable history. Reject visual antiquity when launch brand is new.
3. Chalkdown — https://www.chalkdowncider.com/ — LIVE. Sparkling cider positioned near aperitif/celebration rituals. Useful: category reframing and wine-style serve context; less useful as contemporary interface reference.
4. Krug — https://www.krug.com/ — LIVE. Deeply differentiated champagne editions, blending and gastronomy. Useful: editorial collection storytelling and provenance. Don't borrow luxury house marks or unearned provenance.
5. Dom Pérignon — https://www.domperignon.com/ — LIVE; case-study https://www.commarts.com/webpicks/dom-perignon-1 . Immersive distinct product pages with commerce bridges; useful for paced immersion, not as an excuse to bury utility.
6. Siamo — https://www.siamo.co.za/ — LIVE. South African alcohol-free premium sparkling brand. Useful: local provenance, serving/tasting notes and luxury without alcohol.
7. Mahala Botanical — https://mahalabotanical.com/ — LIVE. South African alcohol-free spirit; ingredient/process proof and product distinctions. Useful: substantiated production story.
8. Ghia — https://drinkghia.com/ — LIVE. Aperitif lifestyle and structured shopping/variant/bundle controls. Useful: commerce with a distinct brand voice and rituals; not minimal luxury.
9. Perricone Farms — https://www.perriconefarms.com/ — LIVE. Premium citrus juice grounded in sourcing/product and B2B use. Useful: ingredient honesty, category truth and clear audience paths.
10. Pressed for Juice — https://pressedforjuice.com/ — LIVE. Distinctive glass bottles, sourcing and positioning; useful for experience/packaging ties.

## Research sampling protocol
For each brief, select at least 2 live category peers, 2 adjacent categories (e.g. champagne + juice), 2 visual-case projects, and 1 contrasting/non-luxury reference. Note what works, what fails, what can't be verified and which principles are safe to abstract. Aim for structural diversity: distinguish campaign landing vs story-led brand, shop-led product, editorial lifestyle, documentary craft and launch/stockist-only. Write an originality rejection list to prevent copying an entire packaging colour scheme, photo arrangement, hero layout or unique motion signature. Never claim visual concepts demonstrate conversion, live responsiveness, SEO or production quality without testing.
